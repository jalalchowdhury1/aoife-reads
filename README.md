# Word Woods 🦉

A reading and spelling game for Aoife, with Ollie the owl.

Six little games — Sound Hunt, Echo Words, Word Snap, Story Gap, Read & Answer, and
Spell It — that start from letter sounds and climb to grade-5 reading. Level 1
("Find Your Reading Powers") finds where she is; later levels build from there.

The parent page at `/parent` shows her progress, including what grade level and
typical age range each skill sits at. She never sees scores — just Ollie, stars,
and stickers.
